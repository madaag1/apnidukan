How we work

We operate as a reseller and order-facilitation business. We do not keep every item in our own inventory. After an order is placed, we source it from the relevant seller or supplier, retain our disclosed commission or margin, and arrange fulfilment to the customer.

Each listing should accurately identify whether an item is original/authorised, unbranded, handmade, customised, thrifted/pre-owned or supplier-fulfilled. We do not sell counterfeit or clone products and do not claim association with any third-party brand unless expressly authorised.
