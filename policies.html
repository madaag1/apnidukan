Returns, replacements & refunds

Because many products are sourced to order, customised, handmade or thrifted, we do not accept returns for change of mind, size preference, personal preference, or a minor variation that was clearly disclosed in the listing.

If you receive a wrong, damaged, materially defective item, or an item with a material colour/description difference, report it to us on the same calendar day as delivery. Email support@apnidukan.com or contact us on WhatsApp with your order number and clear evidence.

For a transit-damage or wrong-item claim, provide one continuous, unedited parcel-opening video showing the sealed package, shipping label, opening, the product, and the issue. This helps us investigate with the seller or courier; it does not limit any rights that cannot legally be excluded.

Do not return an item until we give written return instructions. Where a claim is approved, the item must be sent to the return address we provide. After receipt and inspection, we will arrange a replacement where available, or another appropriate remedy where required by applicable law. We may request return of the original item before dispatching a replacement.

Shipping & delivery

We offer delivery across India and selected international destinations. Orders are usually prepared and handed to the delivery partner within 1–2 business days after we receive and confirm the order. Estimated transit time is generally 3–7 business days; remote areas, villages, small cities, peak periods, customs checks and carrier events can extend delivery to 7–8 business days or longer. Estimates are not guaranteed delivery dates.

Customers are responsible for a complete and accurate delivery address, PIN/postal code and phone number. We are not responsible for delays, loss, re-shipping cost or failed delivery caused by incorrect or incomplete customer-supplied information. Address changes are only possible before dispatch and must be confirmed by us in writing.

International customers are responsible for applicable customs duties, import taxes, brokerage charges and local compliance requirements unless checkout expressly states otherwise.

Product, intellectual-property & image policy

Apni Dukaan does not sell counterfeit, replica or “clone” products. We do not claim an item is made by, authorised by, sponsored by or affiliated with a third-party brand unless that claim is true and can be substantiated. Third-party brand names, logos, designs and marks belong to their respective owners.

Products may be unbranded, handmade, customised, thrifted/pre-owned or fulfilled by independent sellers. An image is illustrative only when the relevant listing clearly says it is a reference image. Actual colour, finish, texture, packaging and small handcrafted details can vary; this is not a general excuse for a material difference from the listing.

Rights holders may report a concern to support@apnidukan.com with the relevant URL, protected work/mark, proof of authority and contact details. We will review it promptly and may disable a listing while investigating.

Orders, pricing & support

We act as a reseller and order facilitator: certain items are sourced from independent sellers after a customer orders. We may cancel an order and issue an appropriate refund if a seller cannot fulfil it, a listing has a genuine pricing/availability error, or fulfilment would be unlawful or unsafe.

Sometimes an item may be successfully ordered by a customer but later sell out in our stock or with the seller before fulfilment. In such situations, our team will contact you directly to confirm whether you would prefer an alternative product of the same value, to share an expected restock timeline, or to process a refund if you do not want an alternative. We will not proceed with dispatch unless we receive your confirmation for the chosen resolution.

These policies do not exclude consumer rights or remedies that cannot be excluded under applicable law. For support, email support@apnidukan.com or WhatsApp us during stated support hours.
